Features

Saving See how

Save from the share sheetFrom the share button in Safari and elsewhere. The save finishes the moment you tap it.
Tags and notes in the share sheetAdd them on the spot, right after saving. Tapped it by mistake? “Undo” takes it back.
Paste a copied URLFrom the button at the top right of the list.
Title fetched afterwardsThe page title is fetched after the item is already saved.
Thumbnail fetched afterwardsThe page image (og:image) is shown only when there is one.
Page text fetched afterwardsThe start of the page text (up to 4,000 characters) is stored too, so search can find it. See how
FaviconsSo you can tell at a glance which site an item came from.
Publication dateShown when the article has one. The date you saved it is never used as a stand-in.
Duplicate detectionTracking parameters and a difference of www. are ignored, so it counts as the same page.

Digging it out See how

SearchTitles, URLs, tags, notes, and page text, all at once.
Recent searchesThe last five searches you ran are offered as suggestions.
Narrow by tagTap a tag above the list and only that tag remains.
TabsAll / Favorites / Unread. Whichever you pick is still there next time.
Unread markOpening an item marks it read. You can set it back from the edit screen.
FavoritesFrom the press-and-hold menu, or by swiping right.
AskAsk in plain words about what you have collected and how the app works. Follow-up questions take the previous exchange into account. It runs on the device and sends nothing out. See how
Summarize an article“Summary”, from the press-and-hold menu. It reads that page's text and nothing else. Meant for deciding whether to read it. See how
Follow-up about the articleAfter the summary you can keep asking about that article. The answers rest on that article's text alone; nothing else you saved is mixed in.
Article conversations are keptThe five most recent article conversations stay on the device. Open them from the Ask screen. They are not sent to iCloud.

Tidying up See how

Edit the titleFor when the fetch failed, or the page handed over a strange title.
TagsAs many as you like. Tags you have used before are suggested. Upper and lower case count as the same.
Rename and delete tagsFrom “Mute” in Settings, a tag can be renamed or removed across every item at once. Deleting a tag does not delete the bookmarks. See how
NotesWrite down why you saved it. Notes are searchable.
ReorderingDrag with a finger. Only while the whole list is shown.
Fetch againSend the app back out for a title it could not get the first time.
Thumbnail, one itemTurn the image off for just this item.
Thumbnails, all at onceTurn thumbnails off across the list, from “Display” in Settings.

Opening See how

Open inside the appTap to open; closing it puts you back at the same spot in the list.
Open in ReaderShows just the text, without ads or clutter. On by default, and switched off under “Display” in Settings. Pages that do not support it open as usual.
Open in the browserPages that need a login or an extension go to Safari, from the press-and-hold menu.

Deleting and hiding See how

Undo a deletion“Undo” appears for a few seconds after you delete. Only if you leave it alone does the item actually go.
Mute by stringRegister a string found in the URL or title, and matching items are hidden.
Mute by tagItems carrying that tag are hidden.
Switch off without deletingA mute rule can be turned off rather than removed, for when you want it back later.
Count of hidden itemsShown beneath the list. Tap it and Settings opens.

Carrying and copying See how

iCloud syncIt shows up on devices signed in to the same Apple Account. There is no sign-up step. See how
Weekly automatic backupEverything is written as JSON into the Kakesu folder in iCloud Drive.
ExportExport the JSON whenever you like. Where it goes is up to you.
ImportRestore from JSON. URLs already present are skipped, never overwritten.

Keeping it from prying eyes See how

App LockOpening the app asks for Face ID / Touch ID, or your device passcode. Off by default.
Grace period on returnComing back from a link you just opened does not trigger it. Only an absence of a minute or more does.
Cover in the App SwitcherThe contents of your list do not appear in the app-switching screen.
No answers while lockedThe Shortcuts “Search” gives no answer while the device is locked, or while App Lock is on. The widget shows only the count while App Lock is on.
What the lock coversIt covers the app’s screens only. The JSON copy in iCloud Drive stays readable from the Files app.

Using it from elsewhere

Unread widgetPuts the unread count on your Home Screen. The small size lists one recent title, the medium size up to three.
Widget while lockedWhile App Lock is on it shows the count only — no titles, no site names.
Shortcuts“Save Bookmark” and “Search Bookmarks” can be called from the Shortcuts app and from Siri.
Shortcuts and muteSearch returns the same results as the list. Muted items are not returned.
Shortcuts while lockedWhile the device is locked, or while App Lock is on, Search gives no answer. Saving still works while locked (being able to throw something in is the whole point).

Everything else

Dark modeFollows the iOS setting.
Text sizeFollows the iOS text size setting.
LanguagesJapanese, English, German, French, Spanish, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
Terms and policyOpen the terms of use and the privacy policy from “About” in Settings.
RequirementsiOS 17.0 or later. Ask and Summary need a device that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later).

For the gestures themselves see the guide; for how data is handled, the privacy policy.
