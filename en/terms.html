Data integrity

Data saved by the App is held on the user's device and in the user's own iCloud. The developer runs no server and holds none of the user's data. The developer therefore cannot restore data on the user's behalf.

Syncing between devices is entrusted to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). A fault or a change in that mechanism may delay syncing or cause it to fail.

Article conversations are neither synced nor backed up; they remain on that device alone. They are lost when the user changes devices or deletes the App.

Once a week the App writes everything saved to a JSON file in the user's iCloud Drive. For anything important, we recommend keeping this copy somewhere else as well. How to do that is described in the guide.
